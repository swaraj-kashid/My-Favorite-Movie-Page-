About the Movie

The story follows Bruce Wayne, also known as Batman, who joins forces with Lieutenant James Gordon and District Attorney Harvey Dent to fight crime in Gotham City.

However, their mission becomes difficult when a dangerous criminal mastermind known as the Joker creates chaos throughout the city.
